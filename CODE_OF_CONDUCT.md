# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our
community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity
and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness
- Being respectful of differing opinions
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- Harassment, trolling, or derogatory comments
- Publishing others’ private information
- Sexualized language or imagery
- Other conduct inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns via GitHub by contacting the repository maintainers privately
(Security Advisory or maintainer contact on the repository profile). Do not post
personal details about others in public issues.

Community leaders will review and respond as appropriate. All complaints will be
reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
